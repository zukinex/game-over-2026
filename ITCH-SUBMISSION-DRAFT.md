## Title

Crystal Golem — Final Boss

## Short description

A 5-minute final boss fight in Decentraland. Walk into the stone arena,
face the 4.5 m Crystal Golem, and bring it down before your HP runs out —
with shardling minions, a dodge dash, charged heavy blows, and a hidden
weakness to discover.

## Gameplay instructions and controls

- Move: WASD (or left joystick on mobile). Look: mouse (or drag on mobile).
- Attack: click / tap the golem. Each hit deals 10 damage (20 once the core
  is exposed). Hold the click to charge a heavy blow: 25 damage (50 with the
  core exposed), then a short cooldown.
- Dodge: press F or tap the DODGE button to dash 3 m away from the golem and
  gain 0.6 s of invulnerability. 3 s cooldown — time it against the red
  circles.
- Shardlings: small crystal minions spawn through the fight and chase you.
  Click them to smash: +100 score, +10 HP.
- Combo: hits chained without taking damage build a combo — every 5 hits
  raises the score multiplier (up to x4). Taking a hit resets it.
- Goal: defeat the Crystal Golem (300 HP) in under 5 minutes.
- Ground Slam: a red circle appears where you stand — get out before it
  detonates.
- Crystal Volley: the golem fires a spread of crystals. Dodge between them.
- Summon Shards (final phase): the golem summons 3 shardlings at once.
- The golem fights in 3 phases and gets faster as its HP drops. In the final
  phase it turns magenta.
- Win: boss HP reaches 0. Lose: your HP reaches 0. Press PLAY AGAIN / TRY
  AGAIN to retry.
