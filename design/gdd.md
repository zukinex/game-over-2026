# Crystal Golem — Final Boss (GAME OVER 2026)

Short game design doc. Owner: Bonyonk (zukinex). Built for the Decentraland
GAME OVER 2026 game jam (itch.io/jam/game-over-2026). Deadline: Nov 30 2026,
23:59 UTC (Dec 1, 06:59 WIB). Prize pool $8,000 in MANA, 10 places.

## The game in one line

You walk into a stone arena, face a 4.5 m Crystal Golem, click it to attack,
dodge its telegraphed attacks, and kill it in under 5 minutes.

## Core loop

1. Spawn at the south edge of a circular arena. Intro panel: goal + controls.
2. Press START. The golem wakes (arms rise, roar text).
3. Click / tap the golem to attack (10 damage per hit, 0.4 s cooldown).
4. Dodge: red circles (ground slam), flying crystals (volley), small shards
   that chase you (click them to smash: +100 score, +5 HP).
5. Boss HP 300. Player HP 100. Win in ~2.5–4 min. Lose = HP 0.

## Boss phases [agent-decided]

- Phase 1 (HP 300–201): Ground Slam only, slow (2.8 s between attacks).
- Phase 2 (HP 200–101): + Crystal Volley, faster (2.2 s).
- Phase 3 (HP 100–0): + Summon Shards, fastest (1.7 s), golem glows magenta.

## Damage numbers [agent-decided]

- Player attack: 10 per click. Boss HP 300 = 30 hits.
- Slam hit: 20. Projectile hit: 10. Shard touch: 10.
- Score: 1000 win bonus + max(0, 300 − seconds) × 10 time bonus + 100/shard.

## What the judges score (7 criteria, equal weight)

Boss Design / Boss Mechanics / Gameplay & Controls / Challenge & Victory /
Creativity & Originality / World Design & Presentation / Technical Quality.
Design choices map to them: glowing crystal golem built from primitives,
3 telegraphed attacks + 3 phases, click-to-attack + dodge, fair pacing with
a victory screen + score, arena + torches + flickering flames, single-parcel
tested build with no external assets.

## Scope decisions [agent-decided]

- 2×2 parcels (32×32 m) instead of 1×1: a 16 m arena is too cramped to
  dodge AoE circles. Worlds have no parcel limit; the RGL World is fresh.
- No downloaded GLB/audio assets in v1: everything is SDK primitives +
  emissive materials, so the scene has zero external dependencies and stays
  far under the World storage budget. Audio is a post-jam polish option.
- Single player, no multiplayer server. No leaderboard (optional per rules).
- Coded HUD (src/hud.tsx), not the editable UI contract: the HUD is
  inherently data-driven (HP bars, timer, score).

## Technical layout

- `assets/scene/main.composite` — static arena + golem (generated by
  `tools/generate-composite.mjs`, edit-mode: inspector::Nodes kept in sync).
- `src/index.ts` — entry point. `src/game.ts` — state machine, boss AI,
  attacks, projectiles, shards. `src/logic.ts` — pure logic (damage,
  phases, scoring; no SDK imports, unit-tested). `src/hud.tsx` — HUD.
- `scene.json` — 2×2 parcels, spawn facing the boss, tags ["game"].
- Deploy target: RGL-assigned World (name TBD from the World Access form).
  `worldConfiguration.name` is added to scene.json once the World is assigned.
  NEVER deploy to the submission World after Nov 30 23:59 UTC: instant DQ.

## Verification

- `npm run build` must exit 0 (type-check + bundle).

- In-world preview: Creator Hub on the owner's PC (`npm run start`).
- Final check before submit: deploy to the RGL World, walk in, play a full
